# Security Policy

## Reporting A Vulnerability

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities through public issues.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository or email <marcus@bawmedical.co.uk>.

Include the issue and impact, affected versions or commit hashes, safe reproduction steps, and suggested mitigation where available. Redact credentials, licensed terminology content, patient-identifiable data, private application rows, and sensitive paths.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days and provide a substantive response within seven business days.

## Scope

This policy covers this repository. Report upstream `sct`, marimo, or other dependency vulnerabilities to the relevant maintainer and notify us privately if they affect this project.

## Safety-Relevant Reports

Flag any issue that could misstate terminology membership, provenance, historical validity, hierarchy completeness, clinical interpretation, or application-data effects. We will triage it as both a security finding and a potential safety hazard.

## Disclosure

We use coordinated disclosure. After a fix is available, we will publish an appropriate advisory or release note and credit the reporter if they agree.
